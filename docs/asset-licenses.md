The chiptune score uses a fixed eight-bar root progression with four melodic sections, triangle bass, square/triangle lead, seeded noise percussion, and synthesized kick drums. Title tempo is 104 BPM, battle tempo is 132 BPM with up to 16 BPM added for intensity, and endings use 108 BPM. Changes occur on bar boundaries. Special effects duck music. Composition arrays and synthesis implementation are included in source; bundled playback needs no network media.
